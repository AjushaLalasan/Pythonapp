Frequently Asked Questions

General Contracting & Construction FAQs

Clear answers regarding our bidding process, warranty guarantees, and site safety management.

How accurate is your preliminary Cost Estimator tool?

The online calculator provides high-level directional feasibility based on current regional construction indices and average material square-foot rates. Once you provide preliminary blueprints, our pre-construction division produces a Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP) with line-item trade breakdowns.

Does BuildPro handle municipal permits and zoning approvals?

Yes, absolutely. Our turnkey general contracting includes submitting all structural calculations, fire department approvals, utility tie-in filings, and securing Certificates of Occupancy with local building departments.

What warranties and safety guarantees do you offer?

All structural concrete, steel foundations, and roofing systems carry a comprehensive multi-year structural warranty alongside standard 1-year master builder craftsmanship warranties. All field teams adhere to OSHA 30 standards with full workers' compensation and \$10M+ general liability policies.
